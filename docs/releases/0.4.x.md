# Engineering changes included in source 0.4.1

These capabilities entered source after v0.4.0 and are included in 0.4.1.
They retain the etlayout namespace, desktop shell and original example IDs.
See the [0.4.1 notes](0.4.1.md) for project opening, provenance and guidance, and
[Releases](https://github.com/mmensing/electrothermal-layout-studio/releases) for
actual downloads. Reviewed source, a local candidate and publication are distinct.

## Compatibility and interpretation

- Empty hard-limit lists, default 50/50 interface heat splitting, unnamed heat
  layers and zero mesh grading retain their canonical legacy identities. Original
  E1–E40 reference inputs remain available; scenarios are explicit alternatives.
- New hard-limit configurations require the updated reader. Older strict readers
  reject unsupported fields rather than silently dropping engineering limits.
  Limit-free schema-2/3 compatibility remains covered.
- Equality or exceedance fails engineering acceptance independently of PDE
  convergence. CLI exit 3 retains result files. An optional transient stop records
  its first violating accepted sample, actual duration and inspectable history;
  this is not an exactly localized threshold-crossing time.
- A cooled displayed frame cannot erase a failed whole-run temperature check.
  Missing whole-run evidence and ambiguous legacy component-local temperatures
  remain unavailable, with recomputation guidance.
- Mesh confidence applies to the actual metric/support/study. Ideal sharp-corner
  raw maxima can remain singular while integral or fixed-area quantities stabilize.
  A faceted radius estimate is not a manufactured-radius or lifetime certification.
- Thermal-only layers do not conduct DC current. Explicit loss placement changes
  where existing electrical power is deposited, not its magnitude; avoid double
  counting an effective gap or substrate contribution.
- Temporal studies share one verified physical mesh across worker processes.
  Assembly and sparse-solve timings are measured subsets, not extra additive
  pipeline phases. A CPU budget does not promise parallel sparse factorization.

[Validation](../validation.md#reviewed-04x-combined-snapshot) records the measured
2,550-test combined source checkpoint, statement/branch coverage and the separate
native/frozen evidence scopes. [Implementation status](../implementation-status.md)

## Remaining boundaries

Material seeds and examples are synthetic/representative, not process calibration
or device ratings. No full 3D comparison, experimental validation, high-field
semiconductor transport, breakdown or switching model is established here.
Capacity/interface/transport laws not checked by a range report stay explicitly
unestablished. The original intermittent Qt/Shiboken GC access violation remains
unisolated; releasing reproduced completed-worker ownership is not proof of its
cause. Clean-machine, hardware monitor-DPI and screen-reader validation remain
unperformed. Publication and third-party licensing gates remain unchanged.
