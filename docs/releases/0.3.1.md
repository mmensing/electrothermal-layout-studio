# Historical engineering notes — 0.3.1

Pre-public application version, 14 October 2026. The earlier distribution was
GPL-3.0-or-later; earlier recipients retain those rights. These notes do not
announce a public tag or binary. Use the [current source edition](../../README.md)
and [owner publication procedure](../maintainer-guide.md).

## Scope

GaN references gained opposing coplanar source/drain combs, insulated upper
redistribution, localized gate pads/runners and a multi-bank gate trunk. SiC
on-state references gained insulated gate routing and nonactive perimeter margins.
Active widths/areas, device parameters and nominal current requests were preserved;
these changes do not add switching, breakdown or gate-controlled SiC physics.

Live exact thermal intersections/exclusions removed avoidable support warnings.
Operating conditions expose physical-port values and A/V units; Discard restores
applied controls, project replacement clears obsolete failures and loaded meshes
remain previewable. Default catalog layer/field selections and numerical map ranges
were corrected. Ambiguous area-plus-edge unions are rejected explicitly.

Larger independent FEM assemblies gained bounded CPU threads and measured worker
provenance. Valid constant operators and compatible sparse factors are reused,
with unchanged physical tolerances and cancellation. Small batches and SuperLU
factorization remain serial.

## Compatibility

Schemas 1 and 2 remain supported. Empty `exclude_sources` preserves older physical
identity; nonempty exclusions require the updated reader. Source masks combine
with the declared operation, excluded masks subtract as a union and explicit
geometry unions afterward. Physical-port-owned landing pulse drives must migrate
to the owning port. Revised layout hashes require recomputed fields; old values
are not relabeled as evidence for new geometry.

## Historical verification

The unfiltered source checkpoint recorded 1,294 tests in 1,465.50 s, 86.26%
statement and 75.94% branch coverage, zero exclusions and stable guarded files.
Native 100%/125% replays inspected all 24 references, all stages and read-only
History/restoration. The frozen candidate passed 25 executable checks including
an engineering E21 5 A calculation with four active assembly workers. These are
historical build-host observations; private Git/CI mappings and bulky evidence
are not public source content. Fresh public checks remain necessary.

E21 and E23 received strict two-level mesh screens at unchanged 5 A/10 A service,
including export/reimport. Independent nominal E20/E22/E24 solves remain distinct
from mesh verification. Dimensions, current sharing, physically averaged hotspots,
temperatures, timings and resource limits are retained in
[the power-example guide](../power-examples.md#current-031-reference-validation)
and its content-identified scientific receipts. No routing winner or experimental
calibration is implied.

One common-mesh CPU pair on historical 0.3.0 E21 geometry measured numerical time
63.851/59.339 s for one/four CPUs, while complete calculation/reporting took
104.216/109.805 s. Four assembly threads were active and all 49 compared fields
were identical. This is evidence of concurrency, not a universal speedup. See
[the maintained receipt](../../benchmarks/e21-cpu-0.3.1.json). Later exact-reporting
improvements are documented separately in [performance](../performance.md).

## Limits

Synthetic models require calibration. Large benchmarks may remain resource-limited.
Native Windows and scaled Qt captures do not establish clean-machine compatibility,
physical-monitor DPI transitions or accessibility certification. No public binary
is supplied here; notices and application source alone do not satisfy all third-party
corresponding-source or LGPL replacement/debugging requirements.
